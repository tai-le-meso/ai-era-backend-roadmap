What I learn
The exactly-once conversationBeing able to explain why exactly-once across Kafka and PostgreSQL requires outbox plus dedupe — and not Kafka transactions — is a genuinely senior distinction that most engineers get wrong.
Interrogating AI's exactly-once claimsModels conflate Kafka transactions with end-to-end exactly-once almost universally. Pushing on 'does that include my database write?' is the question that exposes it.
What I do
  1. Finish the chaos suite and get it green.
  2. Write the Reason answers.
  3. Run the AI drill and push specifically on the database boundary.
  4. Log the result.
What I conclude
  • Reason (write it out): choose the partition key for transfers. What does it guarantee? What goes wrong if one account is 40% of volume? Why is at-least-once + idempotency the right default over Kafka transactions?
  • AI drill: ask AI to 'make this consumer exactly-once'. Interrogate every claim — most answers conflate Kafka transactions with end-to-end exactly-once. Ask directly whether it covers the database write.
  • One precise follow-up question ('does that include my database?') collapses a whole category of confident wrong answers.
What I remember & apply
I can explain why end-to-end exactly-once needs outbox + dedupe, not Kafka transactions → apply: the self-check for this week.
Deliverable: idempotent consumers + retry/DLT + chaos suite → apply: 3B's saga work assumes all of this.
'Does that include my database?' → apply: the standard follow-up on any exactly-once claim.
