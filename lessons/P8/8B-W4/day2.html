Learn II → Design — The capstone: design from a blank page

Architecture Decision-Making, Communication & Capstone · Phase 8 — Solution Architecture · ~4.5h
Day 1Day 2 — Learn II → DesignDay 3Day 4↑ Week overview
What I learn
Options and the decisionThree genuinely different architectures, each with a trade-off table covering the seven review dimensions. Then the decision, with an ADR in the full six-section format including the fair description of the rejected options.
Capacity and costApply the 4B estimation method and the 8A cost model. Numbers, not adjectives. 'Roughly 400 requests per second peak, cost per transaction around X at 10× volume' is what makes the design assessable.
Failure analysisWhat fails, blast radius, recovery, partial-failure behaviour, and the degradation matrix per dependency. This is the section reviews probe hardest and the one most people rush.
What I do
  1. Produce three architecture options with trade-off tables.
  2. Write the ADR with the decision and the rejected options described fairly.
  3. Build the capacity estimate and cost model with real numbers.
  4. Write the failure analysis and degradation matrix.
What I conclude
  • Three genuinely different options is harder than it sounds — two variations of one idea is the common failure.
  • The failure analysis is what a review panel will spend most of its time on.
What I remember & apply
Three genuinely different options, not variations → apply: if they share the same core, you have one option.
Numbers, not adjectives, for capacity and cost → apply: 'scalable' is not a capacity estimate.
Failure analysis gets the most review attention → apply: give it the most preparation.
