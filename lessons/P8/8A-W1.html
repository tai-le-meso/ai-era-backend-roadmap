Topics for today

  • Revisit with 21 months of evidence: Monolith · Modular Monolith · Microservices · Event-driven · Serverless · Cell-based architecture.
  • For each: team topology fit (Conway's Law), deployment independence, failure isolation, data ownership, operational cost, debugging cost, and the honest failure story.
Artifact: notes page committed to logs/. End-of-day question: which of today's topics could you not yet explain to a junior?

Remaining topics

  • The distributed monolith antipattern and how to detect you have built one.
  • Migration paths between styles in both directions (including microservices → modular monolith, which is real).

Design target

Write a comparison document scored against the flagship's actual measured characteristics.

Day 4 — Finish · Measure · Reason · AI~4.5h
Objective: ship the deliverable, then do the thinking that turns the week into senior-level judgment.
0:00–1:30Finish the build: edge cases, break it deliberately, measure.
1:30–3:00Reason (written — never skip): Given your team size (be honest — your real team), which style is correct for the flagship today? What would have to change for the answer to change?
3:00–4:30AI drill: "Give me the strongest argument for the style I did not choose." Then write the rebuttal. This is the exact debate you will have in real reviews. Then log what you accepted, rejected, and why.
