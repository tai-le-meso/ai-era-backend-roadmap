Architecture Design at Scale · Phase 8 — Solution Architecture · ~4.5h
Day 1Day 2 — Learn II → DesignDay 3Day 4↑ Week overview
What I learn
Scoring against measurementsDeployment independence, failure isolation, operational cost, debugging cost, and team fit — scored against what you measured in the flagship rather than against what the articles claim.
Migration in both directionsMonolith to microservices is well documented. Microservices to modular monolith is also real and increasingly common, and knowing it is a legitimate direction removes the sense that decomposition is a one-way ratchet.
What I do
  1. Write docs/architecture/style-comparison.md scored against your measurements.
  2. Verify the distributed-monolith detectors are working and check the flagship.
  3. Document the migration path in both directions.
  4. State the correct style for your actual team today.
What I conclude
  • A style comparison scored against your own measurements is far more useful than a general one.
  • Naming the conditions under which the answer flips is what makes the comparison durable.
What I remember & apply
Score styles against your own measurements → apply: general comparisons do not decide your case.
Document migration in both directions → apply: consolidation is a legitimate move.
