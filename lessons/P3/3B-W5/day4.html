What I learn
Reading a trace waterfallThe skill is finding the critical path — the chain that determines total duration — rather than the longest individual span. Parallel spans can be long without mattering.
AI on trace waterfallsModels tend to pick the longest span rather than reason about the critical path. Testing this specifically is a clean way to see the difference between pattern-matching and reasoning.
What I do
  1. Finish the instrumentation and commit the trace screenshot.
  2. Write the Reason answers.
  3. Run the AI drill on a trace waterfall and compare with your own analysis.
  4. Note whether it reasoned about the critical path.
What I conclude
  • Reason (write it out): take one production-style incident from your own experience. Which span would have shown you the cause? What is missing from your instrumentation?
  • AI drill: give AI a trace waterfall and ask for the bottleneck. Diagnose it yourself too. Note whether AI reasoned about the critical path or just picked the longest span.
  • Critical-path reasoning requires understanding dependency structure, not just reading durations — a good discriminator.
What I remember & apply
I can propagate trace context through a Kafka message manually → apply: the self-check for this week.
Deliverable: connected end-to-end trace across Kafka + correlated logs → apply: 6B adds AI spans to this same trace.
Critical path ≠ longest span → apply: the question to ask of any waterfall, human or AI-analysed.
