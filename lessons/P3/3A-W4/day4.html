What I learn
Per-operation reasoning'Is my system CP or AP' is the wrong question because it is per-operation, not per-system. Pushing a conversation from system-level to operation-level is a senior move.
AI's oversimplification hereModels reliably answer CAP questions at system level with a confident label. Pushing to PACELC and to per-operation reasoning is where the conversation becomes useful.
What I do
  1. Finish the consistency map.
  2. Write the Reason answers.
  3. Run the AI drill and push to per-operation PACELC reasoning.
  4. Note how many prompts it took to get there.
What I conclude
  • Reason (write it out): for each flagship read path, name the weakest consistency you can tolerate and the business consequence of getting it wrong. Balance display vs balance-for-authorisation are different answers.
  • AI drill: ask AI 'is my system CP or AP?'. It will usually oversimplify. Push it to PACELC and to per-operation rather than per-system reasoning.
  • Counting the prompts needed to reach a good answer is a useful proxy for how much the model actually understands versus recites.
What I remember & apply
I can state PACELC and apply it to my own system → apply: the self-check for this week.
Deliverable: consistency map, one row per read path → apply: 4A's bounded contexts and 8A's multi-region work both build on it.
Push system-level questions to operation level → apply: works with models and with people.
