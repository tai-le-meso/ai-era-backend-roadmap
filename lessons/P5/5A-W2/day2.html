What I learn
Fowler's Account/Entry/Transaction shapeAn AccountingTransaction (journal entry) owns 2+ Entries (ledger lines), each referencing an Account and an amount; the transaction constructor refuses to build unless lines balance. Posting Rules turn business events into entries — keeping 'what happened' separate from 'how it books'.
Balance is a derived fact, not a columnThe engineering anti-pattern is a mutable balance column updated alongside inserts: it can drift from the entries under crash, race, or bug, and nothing detects the drift. The ledger's truth is the entry log; balances are projections of it (Week 3 makes the projection fast).
Schema design for the ledger coreTables: ledger_account (id, code, name, normal_side, currency), journal_entry (id, effective_at, description, idempotency_key), ledger_line (id, entry_id, account_id, amount signed or debit/credit typed, currency). Enforce sum-to-zero per entry with a deferred constraint trigger; enforce currency homogeneity per line-vs-account.
Append-only is a grant, not a promiseREVOKE UPDATE, DELETE ON journal_entry, ledger_line FROM app_role — the application physically cannot mutate history even under a bad hotfix. Corrections are new entries (Week 5's reversal/adjustment).
What I do
  1. Read Fowler's accounting paper sections on Transaction/Entry and Posting Rules; note how his AccountingTransaction enforces balance in the constructor.
  2. Read TigerBeetle's debit-credit page; compare its transfer schema with your planned journal_entry/ledger_line schema.
  3. Write the DDL: tables, the sum-to-zero constraint trigger, and the REVOKE statements in a migration.
  4. Design the JournalEntry/LedgerLine Java types: immutable, static factory that throws if lines don't balance or mix currencies.
What I conclude
  • Enforce invariants in three layers: type constructor (fast feedback), DB constraint (last line of defence), property test (catches design gaps).
  • Separating business events from posting rules lets accounting treatment change without rewriting event producers.
  • Signed-amount lines vs debit/credit columns is a representation choice; sum-to-zero is the invariant either way.
What I remember & apply
JournalEntry constructor rejects <2 lines, unbalanced sums, or mixed currencies → apply: static factory + unit tests on day 3.
REVOKE UPDATE/DELETE from the app's DB role = append-only that survives hotfix pressure → apply: the migration you commit today.
Balance column that is mutated in place = undetectable drift; entries are the source of truth → apply: argue this in the day-4 reasoning and 5A-W3.
Posting rules map business events → journal entries, decoupling domain from accounting treatment → apply: fee engine in 5B-W3.
