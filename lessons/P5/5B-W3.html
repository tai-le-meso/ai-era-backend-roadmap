Day 4 — Finish · Measure · Reason · AI~4.5h
Objective: ship the deliverable, then do the thinking that turns the week into senior-level judgment.

1:30–3:00Reason (written — never skip): Where must the limit check happen so it cannot be raced? (Hint: 2A isolation levels + 4A aggregate boundaries.) What is the cost of a false positive to the business, and how should that shape thresholds?
