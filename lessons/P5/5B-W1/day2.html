What I learn
Correspondent banking and nostro/vostro in practiceCross-border payments hop through correspondent banks: each hop is a debit/credit across a nostro/vostro pair, fees may be deducted en route, and Swift messages instruct while the correspondents' books settle. Your 5A-W1 nostro vocabulary becomes operational: an international transfer is a chain of bilateral ledger updates, each with its own timing.
ISO 20022 message anatomyEvery message = Business Application Header + Document. pain.001 = customer→bank payment initiation; pacs.008 = bank→bank customer credit transfer (the interbank workhorse); pacs.002 = payment status report; camt.053/052 = statements/reports; camt.056 = cancellation request. Inside pacs.008: GrpHdr (MsgId, SttlmInf), then CdtTrfTxInf blocks with Dbtr/DbtrAgt/CdtrAgt/Cdtr, IntrBkSttlmAmt, and end-to-end references (EndToEndId, UETR) — the references that make W6-style recon possible.
MT→ISO 20022 migration and PSD2/Open BankingSwift's CBPR+ moved cross-border messaging from MT (MT103) to ISO 20022 (pacs) with structured, richer data (structured-address mandates land Nov 2026) — a live migration you may work on. PSD2 opened bank access (AIS/PIS) via APIs; the UK Open Banking spec from 5A-W1 is its concrete form.
What I do
  1. On iso20022.org, download the pacs.008 message definition report and XSD; read GrpHdr and one CdtTrfTxInf end-to-end.
  2. Map pacs.008 fields to your domain: Dbtr→?, EndToEndId→your reference, IntrBkSttlmAmt→Money; write the mapping table.
  3. Draw a two-correspondent cross-border payment as journal entries across four banks' books (use the 5A-W2 nostro entry pattern).
  4. Design the PaymentRail port: submit(payment) → RailReceipt, plus rail-specific finality metadata, status callback, and return ingestion; sketch the three adapters (card-like, batch, instant/RTGS).
What I conclude
  • One port, three adapters works because rails differ in timing/finality/returns — exactly what the port's metadata must expose, not hide.
  • ISO 20022's end-to-end references (EndToEndId, UETR) are the connective tissue between payments, statements, and reconciliation.
  • A cross-border payment is not one movement but a chain of bilateral nostro updates — explains both the delay and the deducted fees.
What I remember & apply
pain = initiation, pacs = interbank execution, pacs.002 = status, camt = statements/cash mgmt → apply: parser and adapter naming today and in W2.
EndToEndId/UETR travel unchanged across the chain → apply: propagate into your ledger references for recon.
MT103 ≈ pacs.008 across the migration; structured data is the point → apply: talking point + mapping if your employer runs the migration.
Rail port exposes finality semantics as data (finality moment, return window) → apply: day-3 implementation.
