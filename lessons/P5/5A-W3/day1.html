Learn I — Balances: ledger, available, pending

Banking Domain & Ledger Engineering · Phase 5 — Fintech / Core Banking · ~4.5h
Day 1 — Learn IDay 2Day 3Day 4↑ Week overview
What I learn
Three balances, three questionsLedger (booked/posted) balance = sum of settled entries: 'what is legally mine now'. Pending balance = posted plus in-flight entries: 'what it will be if everything lands'. Available balance = posted minus pending outgoing (and minus holds, plus overdraft headroom): 'what can I spend right now without risk of overdrawing'. The app showing two different numbers is correct behaviour, not a bug.
Authorisation holdsA card authorisation reserves amount against available balance without posting to the ledger; it later captures (becomes a posted entry, possibly for a different amount), is voided, or expires after a scheme-defined window (days). Holds therefore need their own lifecycle and an expiry sweeper — TigerBeetle models this as pending transfers with post/void/timeout.
Overdrafts and credit limitsAvailable = posted − holds − pending-out + overdraft limit. The overdraft is a product attribute joined in at check time, not baked into stored balances, because limits change without any money moving.
What I do
  1. Read the Modern Treasury ledger-balances post; write the formula for each of the three balances in terms of your ledger_line and hold tables.
  2. Read TigerBeetle two-phase transfers; map pending/post/void/expire onto your hold lifecycle.
  3. Enumerate 5 scenarios (hold placed, hold expired, capture below hold amount, pending inbound, overdraft granted) and compute all three balances by hand for each.
  4. Write those 5 scenarios as failing acceptance tests against a balance API you will implement day 3.
What I conclude
  • Balances are answers to different questions, so an account exposes a set of typed balances — mirroring what Open Banking APIs already do.
  • Holds are not ledger entries: they affect available balance only, and they expire — someone must own the expiry job.
  • Available balance is the only one you may authorise against; posted balance is the only one you may report as fact.
What I remember & apply
available = posted − holds − pending outgoing + overdraft headroom → apply: the authorisation check in 5B-W3's limits engine.
Capture can settle for less than the hold (partial capture); the residue must release → apply: state machine in 5A-W5.
Holds expire on a scheme window; missing the expiry sweep silently starves customer spending power → apply: scheduled job + metric on active-hold age.
Show posted balance on statements, available balance at point of spend → apply: the PM explanation in this week's check.
