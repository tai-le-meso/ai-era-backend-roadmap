Day 2 — Learn II → Design~4.5h
Objective: finish the theory, then design this block's build before writing any real code.
0:00–1:30Remaining theory (below).
1:30–2:30Design: sketch the approach, decide the tests, name the risks. Ten minutes of diagram beats an hour of rework.
2:30–4:30Scaffold and start: branch, skeleton, first failing test.

Remaining topics

  • Journal entries, T-accounts, the general ledger, sub-ledgers, chart of accounts, trial balance, the rule that every entry must balance to zero.
  • Why software engineers get this wrong: treating balance as a mutable column instead of a derived fact.

Design target

Implement the ledger core: immutable JournalEntry with ≥2 LedgerLines, a database constraint or trigger enforcing sum-to-zero per entry, and append-only enforcement (no UPDATE, no DELETE — revoke the grants, don't just promise).

Artifact: design note + branch with a failing test. End-of-day question: what is the riskiest assumption in your design?

Day 4 — Finish · Measure · Reason · AI~4.5h

1:30–3:00Reason (written — never skip): Model a customer deposit, a withdrawal, a fee, and an inter-bank transfer as journal entries. Which account is debited and which credited, and why?
