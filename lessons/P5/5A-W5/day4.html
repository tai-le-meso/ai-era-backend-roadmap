What I learn
Reversal vs adjustment, to an auditorReversal: 'the transaction should not have happened; here is its equal-and-opposite twin, netting to zero, both visible.' Adjustment: 'the transaction was right in kind, wrong in amount; here is the correcting entry and the reason.' Both preserve the full evidential chain: original, correction, link, reason, actor, timestamp.
Why 'just fix the row' destroys audit integrityConcretely broken: (1) the hash-chain/tamper-evidence you add in W7 fails verification; (2) already-issued statements and trial balances no longer match the data, so you can no longer prove they were ever correct; (3) the customer's and the bank's history diverge; (4) SoD is bypassed — one engineer changed a financial fact with no approval trail. The teachable AI moment: models suggest UPDATE because most CRUD training data is not append-only.
What I do
  1. Run the drill: ask AI to 'fix an incorrect transaction'; if it suggests UPDATE/DELETE, document the correction with the four concrete breakages above; if it doesn't, tighten the prompt until you find its boundary.
  2. Write the auditor-facing one-pager: reversal vs adjustment with a worked example of each from your own ledger data.
  3. Commit deliverables: state machine + reversal/adjustment + as-of-date query.
  4. Self-check: explain reversal vs adjustment to a non-technical person in under a minute (use the 'wrong transaction vs wrong amount' framing).
What I conclude
  • Corrections are new facts about old facts — the ledger's history only ever grows, and that is precisely what makes it evidence.
  • AI defaults to CRUD instincts on financial data; your job is supplying the append-only constraint in the prompt and catching it in review.
What I remember & apply
Auditor framing: reversal = wrong transaction, adjustment = wrong amount, both additive and linked → apply: ops runbook wording.
Four breakages of in-place edits: tamper-evidence, report reproducibility, counterparty divergence, SoD bypass → apply: cite in code review when someone proposes an UPDATE.
Ops tooling gets reverse/adjust buttons, never edit buttons → apply: back-office API design in 5A-W7 (maker-checker).
