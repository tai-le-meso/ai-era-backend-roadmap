Learn I — The banking domain vocabulary

Banking Domain & Ledger Engineering · Phase 5 — Fintech / Core Banking · ~4.5h
Day 1 — Learn IDay 2Day 3Day 4↑ Week overview
What I learn
Party vs Customer vs Account HolderA Party is any legal or natural person known to the bank; a Customer is a party with a contractual relationship; an Account Holder is a party linked to a specific account in a specific role (owner, joint holder, power of attorney). One party can hold many roles across many accounts — modelling these as one 'User' table is the classic fintech data-model bug.
Account types and what they are forCurrent/savings are customer positions. A nostro account is 'our money at another bank' (asset to us); the same account is a vostro from the other bank's view ('their money with us', a liability). Suspense accounts hold amounts you cannot yet attribute (unmatched inbound funds); clearing accounts hold in-flight amounts between systems. Suspense balances should trend to zero — a growing suspense account is an ops incident.
Product vs AccountA product is the commercial template (fees, rates, limits, eligibility); an account is an instance of a product for a customer. Product changes must version, not mutate, because existing accounts keep their contracted terms.
Ledger, Transaction, Payment, Transfer, Settlement are different thingsA payment is an instruction to move money; a transfer is the movement between accounts; settlement is the irrevocable discharge of the obligation between institutions; the ledger is the record of all of it; reconciliation proves the records agree. Conflating 'payment' and 'ledger transaction' makes returns and partial settlement unrepresentable.
What I do
  1. Read the CPMI glossary entries for: settlement, clearing, finality, netting, nostro, correspondent banking, suspense account — write each in one sentence of your own.
  2. Skim Modern Treasury 'What is a Ledger?' and note the ledger vs sub-ledger split.
  3. List every money-related noun used in your current employer's codebase (grep for Account, Transaction, Payment, Balance) and record what each actually means there.
  4. Start docs/domain/banking-glossary.md with 15 terms, each with a one-line definition and the authoritative source.
What I conclude
  • Banking vocabulary is standardised for a reason: two institutions must agree on what 'settled' means before money moves between them.
  • Most fintech modelling bugs are vocabulary bugs — 'transaction' meaning three different things in one codebase.
  • Nostro/vostro is the same account seen from two sides; direction of view decides asset vs liability.
What I remember & apply
Nostro = our account at another bank (our asset); vostro = their account with us (our liability) → apply: correspondent-banking and FX settlement models.
Suspense account = temporary parking for unattributable funds, target balance zero → apply: design an ops alert on suspense ageing.
Party ≠ customer ≠ account holder; one party, many roles → apply: separate Party, Relationship and AccountRole entities in the ER map.
Settlement = irrevocable discharge of the interbank obligation, distinct from clearing (exchange/netting of instructions) → apply: status models in 5B.
