What I learn
Vocabulary drift is where bugs liveWhen your system's 'transaction' means an HTTP request but finance's means a balanced journal entry, every conversation with risk, compliance or a partner bank silently miscommunicates. Cataloguing the drift is cheap insurance.
LLMs drift on banking terms tooAI definitions of settlement, clearing and nostro are frequently colloquial or US-card-centric. The exercise of diffing AI output against the CPMI glossary and ISO 20022 dictionary trains the habit you will need for compliance content in 5B-W4: verify against primary sources.
What I do
  1. Ask AI to define all glossary terms; diff each against the CPMI glossary / ISO 20022 dictionary and record every drift in a 'drift log' section of the glossary.
  2. Write the employer-vs-industry mismatch list: at least 5 terms your current system uses differently, with the bug or miscommunication risk each creates.
  3. Finish and commit docs/domain/banking-glossary.md + ER map (the deliverable).
  4. Self-check: explain nostro/vostro and suspense accounts out loud, unprompted, in under two minutes.
What I conclude
  • Authoritative sources (BIS, ISO) exist precisely because informal definitions diverge; use them as the tie-breaker.
  • The mismatch list between your employer's vocabulary and the industry's is a bug-prediction map — keep it updated.
  • AI is a fast first draft for definitions and an unreliable final authority; the diff-against-source workflow is the repeatable fix.
What I remember & apply
CPMI glossary and the ISO 20022 data dictionary are the tie-breakers for payments vocabulary → apply: cite them in design docs and PR reviews.
Clearing exchanges and nets instructions; settlement discharges obligations; they can be hours or days apart → apply: rail finality matrix in 5B-W1.
Every AI-provided definition gets diffed against a primary source before it enters documentation → apply: the compliance drill in 5B-W4.
A growing suspense balance means unattributed money — page someone → apply: recon break handling in 5A-W6.
