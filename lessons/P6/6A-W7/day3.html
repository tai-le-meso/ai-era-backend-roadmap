What I learn
Writing the framework as a team documentA short decision document your team can apply: the four questions, the six comparison dimensions, and the list of decision types where an LLM is not permitted. This is a genuinely useful artifact few teams have.
The compliance boundaryList explicitly which flagship decisions may never involve an LLM. This list is the seed of 6B's decision-rights policy and 7B's non-delegation constitution.
What I do
  1. Write docs/ai/when-not-to-use-an-llm.md as a team-usable framework.
  2. List the decisions where an LLM is not permitted, with reasons.
  3. Verify the de-LLM'd feature performs as expected in the eval suite.
  4. Commit the framework and the comparison data.
What I conclude
  • A decision framework other people can apply is more valuable than a good decision you made alone.
  • The not-permitted list is short, important, and easier to write now than under pressure.
What I remember & apply
Write the framework for the team, not just yourself → apply: it scales your judgment.
Maintain a not-permitted list for AI decisions → apply: it becomes the 6B decision-rights policy.
