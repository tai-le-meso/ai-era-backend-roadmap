What I learn
Defending the pgvector choiceThe argument is the 4B operational-tax argument: you already run PostgreSQL, so pgvector adds no new datastore. A dedicated vector database must beat that by enough to justify backup, monitoring, upgrades and expertise.
Deciding from your numbersBoth sides of this debate are reasonable in general; only your measured recall, latency and corpus size decide it for your system.
What I do
  1. Finish the retrieval implementation and the ADR.
  2. Write the Reason answers.
  3. Run the AI drill and decide from your own measurements.
  4. Commit the recall measurements alongside the ADR.
What I conclude
  • Reason (write it out): what recall are you actually getting, and what does the loss cost you? Why does post-filtering break your top-k? What is the storage and index-build cost at 10× corpus?
  • AI drill: 'Should I use a dedicated vector database instead of pgvector?' Make AI argue both sides, then decide from your measured numbers, not its preference.
  • When both general arguments are reasonable, only local measurement decides — and that is exactly when models are least useful and most confident.
What I remember & apply
I can explain the pre-filter vs post-filter trade-off → apply: the self-check for this week.
Deliverable: pgvector retrieval + recall measurement + ADR-0009 → apply: W4 tunes it, W5 measures it end to end.
Decide from local measurement when general arguments tie → apply: the recurring pattern of Phase 6.
