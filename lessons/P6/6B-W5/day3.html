What I learn
Testing the monitoringSwap in a weaker model and see whether your monitoring notices before you tell it to. If it does not, the monitoring is decorative. This is the AI equivalent of the 3B game day.
Rollback when nothing changed on your sideIf quality drops and you deployed nothing, the plan is: pin the model version if the provider supports it, roll back the prompt version, or switch provider. Having the plan written is what makes a bad morning survivable.
What I do
  1. Simulate a quality regression by swapping in a weaker model.
  2. Verify the monitoring detects it within your target window.
  3. Fix the monitoring if it did not.
  4. Write the rollback plan for provider-side degradation.
What I conclude
  • Monitoring you have not seen fire is monitoring you should not trust.
  • The 'nothing changed on our side' scenario is unique to AI dependencies and needs its own plan.
What I remember & apply
Simulate a regression to test monitoring → apply: the AI equivalent of a game day.
Rollback plan: pin version, revert prompt, switch provider → apply: write it before the morning you need it.
